# Colibly — Logo Guidelines (Nexo)

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** Geometric **C** (Colibly) open to the east + equilateral triangle pointing forward — connection and next step (“Nexo”).
- **Versions:** symbol on Laguna tile (app/favicon) · symbol alone (black / white / Laguna `#0E7490`) · lockup = symbol + wordmark “Colibly” (Newsreader / `font-display`)
- **Files:** `tmp/logo-design/kit/` (SVG + PNG + `favicon.ico`). App wiring: `BrandMark`, `lib/brand-mark.ts`, `app/icon.svg`.

## 2. Clear space
Keep clear space of **½ the stroke width of the C** (≈ the gap between C and triangle) on all sides of the symbol.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol on tile | 16 px (use small cut / favicon) | 6 mm |
| Lockup | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |

Below 24 px prefer the tile version or `colibly-nexo-small.svg`.

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Laguna | `#0E7490` | Primary / tile / mono mark |
| Laguna ink | `#0C5F75` | Text on light |
| On accent | `#FFFFFF` | Mark on Laguna tile |
| Ink | `#0C1A22` | Wordmark on light |

**Approved pairs:** white Nexo on Laguna · Laguna Nexo on white/`#F0F9FF` · black Nexo on white · white Nexo on ink.

## 5. Typography
- Display / wordmark: **Newsreader** (semibold)
- Body: **Figtree**
- Reserve (not in production): Syne + DM Sans — see `docs/TIPOGRAFIA.md`
- Do not recreate the symbol by typing a “C”.

## 6. Don’ts
Don’t stretch · don’t recolour outside Laguna · don’t rotate the triangle · don’t drop the triangle · don’t add shadows/gradients · don’t place on busy photos without a Laguna tile.
