## Brand and public site (since 2026-09-25; renamed to Colibly 2026-09-27)

The product is **Colibly** (domain `colibly.com`). History: SundayCRM → SunflowCRM (sundaycrm.com belonged to someone else) → Colibly (2026-09-27, see `docs/PLAN_LANZAMIENTO.md`). The name and domain live in `lib/brand.ts` (`BRAND_NAME`, `BRAND_TAGLINE`, `BRAND_DOMAIN`, `BRAND_CONTACT_EMAIL`) — use them in new user-facing text instead of a literal; the public URL of the app still comes from `APP_URL` at runtime (`lib/app-url.ts`), so dev keeps pointing at localhost. Everything is named Colibly since 2026-09-27: GitHub repo `ludaisca/Colibly`, folder `/data/Proyectos/Colibly`, npm package, compose project `colibly` (pinned with `name:` in the override), database `colibly_db`. Only history keeps the old names (applied migrations, `prisma/baseline/`, audits) plus the seed users `@sundaycrm.test` in the dev database; the webchat widget reads its old `localStorage` key `sundaycrm_webchat_token_*` once and migrates it to `colibly_webchat_token_*`. Webhook headers are `X-Colibly-Signature` / `X-Colibly-Event` / `X-Colibly-Delivery` (automation webhook steps send `X-Colibly-Automation`), User-Agent `Colibly-Webhooks/1.0`; the data export format is `colibly-export-v1`.
- **Logo (Nexo, 2026-10-25)**: geometric C + east-pointing triangle on the teal accent (`#0F6E7E` since 2026-11-14) — `BrandMark` in `app/components/ui/logo.tsx` (token colors, used by `Logo` and the app shell) and the same shape in fixed colors in `lib/brand-mark.ts` (`app/icon.svg`, `app/apple-icon.tsx`, the share image). The old exports and concept files (`tmp/logo-design/`) and the `/marca` labs were removed on 2026-11-08; they live in git history before that date, and `lib/brand-mark.ts` is the master now.

- **Pricing copy** comes only from `app/(marketing)/_landing-offer-data.ts` (built from `lib/billing/licenses.ts` + `plans.ts`); `colibly.com` is this Next app — the cPanel copy was removed on 2026-11-05.
- **Public site** is the `(marketing)` route group: `/` (landing), `/precios`, `/contacto`, `/eliminar-datos` (public deletion instructions — the URL to register in Meta; since 2026-11-12 `noindex,follow` and out of the sitemap so it doesn't compete in brand searches, with no `Disallow` in robots so Google can see the noindex; the status page `/eliminacion-de-datos/[code]` is separate and also noindex), `/terminos`, `/privacidad`. None of them are in `proxy.ts` lists, but the app host redirects them to the site host. Registration is OPEN (decision 2026-10-18): CTAs point to `/register`, prices come only from `lib/billing/plans.ts`; the beta application form is just an optional contact. A new user gets a welcome email (`welcomeTemplate`) when the email is first verified. `app/not-found.tsx`, `error.tsx` and `global-error.tsx` are the Spanish 404/500 screens. **The landing never calls `auth()`**: `proxy.ts` redirects a signed-in user from `/` to their panel (`/whatsapp/chat` for ejecutivo, `/dashboard` otherwise). Below `md` the header shows a hamburger menu (`app/components/ui/site-mobile-nav.tsx`, shared with the beta layout) instead of the section links; the main call to action is the `CTA` constant in `page.tsx` — reuse it so every button says the same thing.
- **Measurement** (`lib/analytics/`, `app/components/analytics/analytics.tsx`): Meta Pixel, GA4 and Google Ads load **only after the visitor accepts the cookie notice** (choice in the first-party cookie `colibly_consent` — `Domain=colibly.com` on `colibly.com`/`*.colibly.com`, so the public site and `app.colibly.com` share one choice — mirrored in `localStorage`; "Preferencias de cookies" in the footer clears both. GA4 gets `cookie_domain: colibly.com` there, and `?ga_debug=1` turns on DebugView. Campaign params (`utm_*`, `gclid`, `fbclid`…) are copied onto any link to `/register` by a click listener in `<Analytics>` (`withCampaignParams`), since `proxy.ts` keeps the query on the 308 to the app host) and only when their ids exist in `PlatformSettings` (`metaPixelId`, `gaMeasurementId`, `googleAdsId` + `googleAdsSignupLabel` — configured from `/plataforma`, validated formats, `lib/analytics/config.ts:getAnalyticsConfig()`) — without them there's no banner and no third-party script. Mounted in the `(marketing)` and `(auth)` layouts (registration is the conversion), never in the panel. Events: elements with `data-track="cta"` + `data-track-location` (clicks), `#precios` in view, `<details data-track="faq">` opened, and `sign_up` after registration. The register page sends the same event id in `x-analytics-event-id` (only with consent) so `POST /api/auth/register` reports `CompleteRegistration` through Meta's Conversions API (`meta-capi.ts`, needs `metaCapiAccessToken` in `PlatformSettings`) and Meta deduplicates it with the Pixel's. This is Colibly's own ad measurement — unrelated to `lib/meta/conversions.ts` (customers' ad accounts).
- **CSP** (`next.config.ts`): global and strict (`'self'`), with the third-party origins the public site needs listed explicitly (Meta, Google Analytics/Ads, Cloudflare Turnstile). Until 2026-09-27 it only allowed `'self'`, so the browser blocked the Pixel/gtag scripts and beacons even with the ids configured. A new third-party script or beacon needs its origin added there — verify in the browser (`securitypolicyviolation`), `tsc` won't tell you.
- **Signup captcha** (`lib/turnstile.ts`, `app/components/ui/turnstile.tsx`, shared action in `lib/turnstile-shared.ts`): Cloudflare Turnstile, optional — required on `/register` only when both keys are configured in `PlatformSettings` (`turnstileSiteKey`/`turnstileSecretKey`, from `/plataforma`; `register/page.tsx` is an async server wrapper that awaits the site key and passes it to `_form.tsx`). `POST /api/auth/register` verifies the token before touching the DB, and `lib/turnstile.ts` requires `success === true`, the stable action `signup` (the widget sends it and siteverify returns it) and the frontend hostname: production accepts only the `APP_URL` host and fails closed without it, development also accepts `localhost`, `127.0.0.1` and single-label Tailscale names. A rejected/missing token is 400, but if Cloudflare itself can't be reached it lets the signup through (logged) — the per-IP limits still apply. `remoteip` goes along when known. Cloudflare's test keys (site `1x00000000000000000000AA`, secret `1x0000000000000000000000000000000AA` always passes / `2x0000000000000000000000000000000AA` always fails) are handy for local testing, pasted into `/plataforma`.
- **Landing (2026-11-08, rama `feat/landing`)**: mundo Andén en registro de venta, dirección "la página es una conversación" elegida con Impeccable (contrato en `.impeccable/surfaces/app-marketing-page-tsx.md`). Le habla primero al dueño que atiende solo; los equipos tienen su sección más abajo. Piezas:
  - `_thread.tsx` (`Thread`, `QuickReply`): burbujas del panel; los mensajes llegan en orden con su "escribiendo…" en la misma celda (no hay salto de diseño). La portada arranca sola (`data-seq="auto"`, solo CSS); los tramos de abajo los arma `_seq-play.tsx` al entrar en vista y nunca ocultan lo que ya se ve. Sin JS o con "reducir movimiento" todo queda escrito. CSS en `globals.css` → "Hilo de la landing". La acción principal (respuesta rápida) nunca se anima.
  - `_demos.tsx`: bandeja "Esperando" con cifra de espera y la ruta de la venta, estáticas y con datos de ejemplo rotulados; `_faq.tsx`: preguntas como respuestas rápidas (`<details>` nativo).
  - Toda la página son tramos del mismo hilo: portada, bandeja, toma de control, ruta, equipo (notas de sistema), pasos (mensajes numerados), precio (burbuja + "Compara los planes" antes de las tarjetas), preguntas y cierre (un mensaje esperando con su lámpara ámbar y la acción principal como respuesta rápida).
  - Destacado en tinta (plan recomendado, selector de planes); teal solo para actuar. Se quitaron la ventana promocional (interrumpía sin necesidad), el tour/maqueta del panel viejo, el titular animado, los halos y el revelado por sección.
  - Nada de testimonios, logos ni cifras de uso (`PRODUCT.md`): solo precios y prueba reales.
- **Landing chat**: `LANDING_WEBCHAT_ACCOUNT_ID` = a WEBCHAT account of Colibly's own organization (its allowed origins must include the public domain); the `(marketing)` layout loads its `widget.js`. Leads who write there land in that organization's inbox.
- **SEO and sharing**: `metadataBase` comes from `APP_URL` in the root layout's `generateMetadata()` (with `connection()`, because the domain only exists at runtime), so canonical, Open Graph and the file-based images resolve to the real domain. Files: `app/icon.svg` (favicon), `app/apple-icon.tsx`, `(marketing)/opengraph-image.tsx` + `twitter-image.tsx` (both render `_share-image.tsx` — since 2026-11-08 in Andén with Barlow loaded from `public/fonts/` (OFL, `OFL-Barlow.txt`; `public/` is what the Dockerfile copies) — fixed palette colors), `app/robots.ts` and `app/sitemap.ts` (`force-dynamic` for the same reason). The home `<title>`/og/twitter title is a short search title (`Colibly — CRM de WhatsApp con IA en México`, since 2026-11-12: the tagline version was cut off in Google); the visible copy keeps `BRAND_TAGLINE`. The landing emits JSON-LD (`SoftwareApplication` with the plans + `FAQPage`). Since 2026-11-09 the whole public site also emits `Organization` + `WebSite` JSON-LD (`lib/seo/site-schema.ts`, from `(marketing)/layout.tsx`; `/precios` adds a `BreadcrumbList`) and `app/llms.txt/route.ts` serves an `llms.txt` summary for AI assistants whose prices/quotas come from `plans.ts`/`licenses.ts` (the Organization `logo`/`image` is `public/logo-512.png`, a 512×512 solid-background PNG made from `app/icon.svg` by `node scripts/make-logo-png.mjs` run in the app container — regenerate it if the logo changes; the favicon stays `app/icon.svg`; `sameAs` lists the official profiles from `SOCIAL_PROFILES` in `site-schema.ts` — Instagram, Facebook and Trustpilot today; the `contactPoint` carries the support email and, since 2026-11-12, `telephone` = the support WhatsApp (`BRAND_SUPPORT_WHATSAPP` in `lib/brand.ts`, also shown on `/contacto` and in the public footer — a fixed constant on purpose, separate from the in-app `SUPPORT_WHATSAPP` env of `/ayuda`, though both hold the same number); add LinkedIn/Google Business there and it shows site-wide; no address is invented). **`/llms.txt`** groups Guías, Comparativas and Blog (every published post) straight from `_guides.ts` and `content/blog/`, and **`/llms-full.txt`** (`app/llms-full.txt/route.ts`) serves the full Markdown of the home, `/precios`, guides, comparisons and posts from `lib/seo/public-pages.ts` — rendered per request (cached 1 h), not at build, because `SITE_URL` is runtime and scheduled posts must appear on their date without a deploy; the home FAQ and the pricing notes live in `(marketing)/_landing-faq.ts` / `_pricing-notes.ts` so the pages and that file read the same text. **IndexNow**: the public key (`INDEXNOW_KEY` in `lib/seo/indexnow-rules.ts`) is served from `public/<key>.txt` (a test checks both match); the `seo-indexnow` queue (see `colas.md`) POSTs to `api.indexnow.org` the URLs whose Markdown hash changed since the last notice (at app start and daily, production + public domain only). A new kind of public page must be added to `getPublicPages()` to be picked up by both. Search-intent guides (14: `/crm-whatsapp`, `/chatbot-whatsapp-ia`, Coexistence, app-vs-API, API cost, automatic replies, appointments, four sectors, and three comparisons under `/comparativas/alternativa-a-{leadsales,manychat,kommo}` — a guide with `section: "comparativas"`; use `guidePath(g)` for its URL and put its `page.tsx` under `app/(marketing)/comparativas/<slug>/`). **Comparisons name real companies: say only what Colibly does, never state a competitor's price, term or feature as fact (the tables are "what to ask" columns), and keep an honest "when to pick the other one" section** are data in `(marketing)/_guides.ts` rendered by `_guide-page.tsx` (FAQPage + Article + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD) and listed in the `/guias` hub. A new guide = one entry in `_guides.ts` (set `updated`, it feeds `lastmod` and `dateModified`) **plus** a 3-line `page.tsx` in a folder named after the slug (`lib/seo/__tests__/guides.test.ts` fails if it's missing); it enters the sitemap, `llms.txt` and the home/footer links on its own. A root `[slug]` dynamic route was tried and dropped: it makes `@next/next/no-html-link-for-pages` flag every `<a>` in the site. Claim only what the product does today (appointment reminders go to the assigned person, not to the client; the bot does not schedule appointments yet). **In dev, Next always points og:image to `localhost:PORT`** regardless of `metadataBase` (by design) — check share URLs on a production build. Prices and plan features come from `lib/billing/plans.ts` + `lib/billing/plan-features.ts` (shared with `/configuracion/plan`), so a limit change updates both. **Blog** (`colibly.com/blog`, plan in `docs/PLAN_BLOG_2026-11-09.md`): posts are Markdown files in `content/blog/<slug>.md` (frontmatter: `title` ≤ 60, `description` ≤ 160, `date`, `updated`, `category` from the closed list in `lib/blog/posts.ts`, `related`, optional `image`+`imageAlt`, `draft`), read from disk on each request by `lib/blog/posts.ts` — **the Dockerfile `runner` stage has `COPY content`**; without it the blog 404s in production. A post with a future `date` or `draft: true` is hidden (publishing is scheduled by date, no deploy), and it must open with `**Respuesta corta:**`, link to a guide and `/precios`, and have ≥ 450 words (`lib/blog/__tests__/posts.test.ts` enforces it, and that every internal link exists). Body is rendered by `react-markdown` (no raw HTML) in `blog/_markdown.tsx`; pages: `/blog` (`?pagina=`), `/blog/[slug]`, `/blog/categoria/[cat]` (`noindex` and out of the sitemap under `MIN_POSTS_TO_INDEX` = 3 posts), `/blog/rss.xml`, and the share image `/blog/og/[slug]` (a route, not an `opengraph-image` file, so its URL is known for the JSON-LD). Do **not** add a `loading.tsx` under `blog/`: the shell is flushed before `notFound()` runs and unknown slugs would answer 200 instead of 404. `/blog` and `/guias` on `app.*` redirect to the site (`proxy.ts`). In `<a href="/blog">` use `next/link`: that exact path trips `@next/next/no-html-link-for-pages`.
- **Legal texts are drafts**: `app/(marketing)/_legal.tsx` holds the company data (`LEGAL`; responsible party **Colibly Inc.** since 2026-11-05, no CFDI is issued) and the "borrador" notice; both pages mark open questions with `<Blank>`. They must be reviewed by a lawyer and the placeholders filled before public launch.
- **Onboarding checklist** on `/dashboard` (admins only, hidden once complete): `lib/onboarding.ts:getOnboardingSteps()` derives each step from real data (email verified, a channel, a bot or automation, a second member or invite, an ACTIVE plan) — there's no manual "mark as done".
